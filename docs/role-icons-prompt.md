# Codex Model Finder — role icon prompt

> Use case: ui-mockup
> Asset type: a single horizontal three-icon role sprite for a compact fantasy MMORPG party-finder interface.
> Primary request: Create exactly three separate classic early-2000s fantasy MMORPG role icons arranged left-to-right in three equal square cells: (1) TANK — a sturdy silver-blue shield, (2) HEALER — a luminous green-white medical cross or healing star, (3) DPS — a sharp red-orange sword. Each icon should be a simple readable symbol inside its own tiny dark iron circular medallion with a thin worn gold rim.
> Style/medium: hand-painted game UI icon, slightly weathered metal, bold silhouette, readable at 14 pixels, consistent with a dark gunmetal and red-button dungeon-finder panel.
> Composition/framing: one straight horizontal strip, exactly three equal icons, perfectly centered in their cells, generous transparent separation between medallions, no overlap.
> Color palette: blue-silver tank, green-white healer, red-orange damage; dark charcoal iron and muted gold rims.
> Constraints: genuinely transparent background; exactly three icons; no letters, no role names, no numbers, no extra symbols, no watermark, no outer panel, no glow outside each medallion.

The generated strip was split into three transparent 128 px role assets and placed immediately before each role label.
